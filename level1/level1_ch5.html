    4🗺️ Plan

    Two boxes, two dice rolls, one motor block. The loop does the rest.

    🗺️ Your game plan

    1. 📦Make two boxesOne for the left speed, one for the right.
    2. 🎲Roll for the left wheelAny number from -100 to 100.
    3. 🎲Roll for the right wheelA separate roll — that is why it turns.
    4. ⚙️Give the two numbers to the wheelsThen round the loop and roll again.
    5. ⏱️Hold that pair for one secondWithout the wait, new numbers arrive about 50 times a second and the car only shivers.

    5🔨 Build

    ▶ Open MakeCodeKeep it open beside this page — you will build the blocks there, step by step.

    0 of 5 steps done

    1. Add the Cutebot extension: Extensions (just below Math) → search "Cutebot".
    2. In on start, show an icon and make two variables for the wheel speeds.
    3. In a forever loop, roll a random number for each wheel.
    4. Still in the loop, hand both numbers to the motors, then hold them for one second.
    5. Download, unplug, switch on, and put it down in a clear space.
