4🗺️ Plan

Before any code, get the car ready. Four steps, in this order — the last one is the only one that touches the computer.

🗺️ Your game plan

🎯 What it makes
🗺️ The plan
  1. 🔋Put the batteries inThree AAA batteries in the holder. The car needs 3.5 to 5 volts.
  2. 🔌Plug the micro:bit inScreen facing up, into the slot at the back.
  3. 🔎Check it overNo loose screws. Sonar pins straight. Nothing on the base plate.
  4. ⬇️Download your programCable to the computer, then unplug and put the car down.

🚗 Drive test

The face should stay on the screen with the cable unplugged. That proves the program is living in the micro:bit now, not in the computer.

💡

If the screen stays dark, check the batteries before you touch the code. Weak batteries look exactly like a bug.
