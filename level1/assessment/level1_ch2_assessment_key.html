📝 Notes for this chapter

Floor, not desk — the car does not stop on its own in this chapter. That is on purpose; the wiki's own question at the end asks students how to stop it. Fix It answer: the drive block is in on start, so the car takes off the instant it is switched on. Source: ElecFreaks wiki, Case 01 "Move Forward or Reverse at the Full Speed".

🚀 Extend — where each task goes

🟢 The wiki asks this one: how do you make the car stop after you press A?
Press A and watch — the car never stops on its own → Look through every block you have met so far → Decide which one could switch the motors off → If none of them can, say what you would need🟡 Make the car drive away and come back past where it started — you may use both buttons, and you will have to switch it off to end the run.
Press A and let it run → Count out loud while it drives → Press B at the same count → Switch it off when it passes your starting line🔴 Buttons A and B are taken. Give A+B pressed a job of its own, and say why that job suits a third button.
Pick a job the other two do not do → Add on button A+B pressed → Put your blocks inside → Test all three buttons

🧩 The blocks you need today

input_on_button

Runs the blocks inside only when someone presses that button.

input.onButtonPressed(Button.A, function () {
    basic.showIcon(IconNames.Yes)
})

You see: Nothing until you press A on the simulator board. Then a tick mark appears.

⚠ There is a third choice besides A and B: A+B means both at once. Students find it by accident and then cannot explain it.

cutebot_forward

Both wheels start turning forward, and they keep turning.

cuteBot.forward()
basic.pause(2000)
cuteBot.stopcar()

You see: The car drives straight ahead for two seconds, then stops and stays stopped.

⚠ This block only starts the wheels — it does not say how far or how long. Without a stop block the car drives until the batteries die or it hits a wall. Students who leave out the stop block think their program 'crashed'.

cutebot_backward

Both wheels turn the other way, so the car reverses.

cuteBot.backforward()
basic.pause(1000)
cuteBot.stopcar()

You see: The car backs up for one second.

⚠ The function is spelled backforward in the editor — that is the extension's own name, not a typo the student made.

Talk rubric

Strong — says to drive on the floor and names the reason (nothing in this program stops the motors, so the car keeps going). Developing — says to use the floor without saying why the car does not stop. Needs Focus — blames the car or the code being broken.
