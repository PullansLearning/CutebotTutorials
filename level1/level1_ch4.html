📋Check-up

1🔥 Warm-Up

Your car has driven straight and it has crawled. It has never turned. There are turn blocks in the Cutebot drawer, but they just stop one wheel and swing the car round it. The wide curve you want is not a command on this robot: it is what happens when one wheel runs faster than the other. Today you make two turns in a row, opposite ways, and the car draws a figure eight.

👀 Watch first

⬆️ Straight↪️ Left wheel faster⬆️ Straight↩️ Right wheel faster

📚 Words to know

⏱️
pause
Wait this many milliseconds before the next block.
⚡
millisecond
One thousandth of a second. 1000 of them make one second.
↩️
turn
One wheel faster than the other. The turn blocks stop one wheel completely.
🔁
pattern
The same set of moves, repeated.

🌍 In the real world

A digger turns the same way. No steering wheel — one track runs faster than the other, and the machine swings round.

    4🗺️ Plan

    The eight is four moves in a row. Two of them are the same straight; the other two are mirror images of each other.

    🗺️ Your game plan

    🎯 What it makes
    🗺️ The plan
    1. ⬆️Short straightFull speed, 200ms. This is the middle of the eight.
    2. ↪️First loopLeft wheel faster than right, 1000ms.
    3. ⬆️Short straight againThe same 200ms as before.
    4. ↩️Second loopNow the right wheel is the faster one — the mirror of the first.

    5🔨 Build

    ▶ Open MakeCodeKeep it open beside this page — you will build the blocks there, step by step.

    0 of 5 steps done

    1. Add the Cutebot extension: Extensions (just below Math) → search "Cutebot".
    2. Put a show icon block in on start.
    3. In a forever loop, drive straight for 200ms, then curve one way for a second.
    4. Still inside the forever loop, add the second half — the same straight, then the mirror curve.
    5. Download, unplug, switch on, and set the car down in the middle of your space.

    🚗 Drive test

    Watch two full eights. If the second loop is bigger than the first, your two speed pairs are not mirrors — swap the numbers and run it again.

    💡

    If your eight comes out lopsided, the two curves are not mirrors. Check the number pairs are swapped exactly: 100 and 40, then 40 and 100.
