1🔥 Warm-Up

Last chapter your car woke up and showed a face. It still has not moved. Today it moves — and it moves because you press a button, not because the program reaches that line. Blocks under on button A pressed sit and wait. They run when you press the button and let go, and not before.

📚 Words to know

🅰️
button A
One of the two buttons on the front of the micro:bit.
⚡
event
Something that happens, like a button being pressed.
⬆️
forward
Both wheels turn the same way, so the car goes straight ahead.
⬇️
reverse
Both wheels turn the other way, so the car backs up.
🏁
full speed
The fixed, fast speed these two blocks use. Later you will set each wheel's speed yourself.

🌍 In the real world

A lift button does nothing until a finger touches it. The program inside the lift is already running — it is waiting.
