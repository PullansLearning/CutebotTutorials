1🔥 Warm-Up

Last chapter the probes said "table" or "no table" — one question, two answers. Black tape gives the same reading as the edge did, but today the program reads it as the line — and says something richer. Left and right each see black or white, and those two answers combine. The car reads the combination and steers itself back onto the line, over and over, all the way round the track.

📚 Words to know

🛤️
line following
Steering by what the two probes see under the car.
🔲
combination
Left and right together — four possible answers.
↔️
correction
A small steer back towards the line.
〰️
drift
Sliding off the line a little at a time.

🌍 In the real world

Warehouse robots follow taped lines on the floor exactly like this. The tape is cheap; the robot needs no map at all.
