1🔥 Warm-Up

Your car has read light. Now it reads the floor. Two probes underneath shine down and measure what comes back. A table sends light back; the empty air past the edge does not. That difference is all the car needs to save itself.

📚 Words to know

🔎
line sensor
A probe underneath that tells black from white.
🪤
edge
Where the table stops. To the sensors it looks like nothing there.
↩️
escape
The set of moves that gets the car out of trouble.
🔲
state
What the two probes see together — there are four combinations.

🌍 In the real world

A robot vacuum has the same probes and the same rule. That is why it can clean the top of a staircase and not fall down it.

    4🗺️ Plan

    Ask one question over and over. Almost always the answer is "still on the table" — the interesting part is the other branch.

    🗺️ Your game plan

    1. 🔎Ask what the probes seeBoth on the surface, or not.
    2. ⬆️On the table: creep forwardSlow, so there is time to stop.
    3. ↩️Off the edge: reverse firstGet both wheels back on solid ground.
    4. 🎲Then turn a random amountA different escape each time, so it cannot get stuck.

    5🔨 Build

    ▶ Open MakeCodeKeep it open beside this page — you will build the blocks there, step by step.

    0 of 5 steps done

    1. Add the Cutebot extension: Extensions (just below Math) → search "Cutebot".
    2. Put a show icon block in on start.
    3. In a forever loop, creep forward while both probes see the table.
    4. Add the escape in the else branch — reverse, pause, random turn, pause, stop, wait a second.
    5. Download, unplug, switch on, and set it down in the middle of the table.

    🚗 Drive test

    Let it reach the edge three times with your hand ready. Count how many escapes look different. If they all look the same, the random block is not where you think it is.

    💡

    Creep, do not race. At full speed the car is over the edge before the probes have said anything.
