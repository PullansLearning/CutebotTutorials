Before you drive

The kit

Every station runs on a real Cutebot. The MakeCode simulator can’t show it, so there is no screen-only way through. One car is enough for 2⁠–⁠3 students.

  • micro:bit V2The brain
  • CutebotThe car
  • 3 × AAA batteriesUse fresh ones. Weak batteries make the car slow or pull to one side, like a bug in your code.
  • USB cableLoads your code onto the micro:bit
