1🔥 Warm-Up

The two silver circles on the front of your car are not eyes. One makes a sound too high for you to hear; the other listens for it coming back. Time the echo and you have a distance. Today the car uses that number to get itself across a room without touching anything.

👀 Watch first

📡 Ping📏 Get centimeters🎯 Between 2 and 20?↪️ Turn away

📚 Words to know

📡
sonar
Sends out a sound and times the echo to measure distance.
📏
centimeter
The unit the sonar reports back in.
🔗
and
Both tests must be true, or the whole thing is false.
🎯
band
Between a low number and a high one — here, 2 to 20 cm.
🧱
obstacle
Anything in front the car has to get around.

🌍 In the real world

Parking sensors work exactly this way. So do bats — the same trick, several million years earlier.

    🚗 Drive test

    Put a book in its path. It should turn before touching it. Then take the book away and check it goes straight again — the turn must not stick.

    💡

    Store the reading in a variable once each loop. If you call the sonar twice in one test you get two different measurements and the answer stops making sense.
