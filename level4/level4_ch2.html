1🔥 Warm-Up

Last chapter one number split the world in two: near or far. That was enough to dodge a wall, but it can never make a car hold station. With one line there is nowhere to rest — the car is always on one side of it. Today you use two lines and get a band in between, and inside that band the right answer is to do nothing.

    4🗺️ Plan

    Three answers, and only one of them can happen each time round. Ask them in order — the order is what makes the middle band exist.

    🗺️ Your game plan

    1. 📡Measure the gapOne reading into the Sonar box.
    2. ⬇️Under 5cm? ReverseToo close — get out of the way.
    3. ⏸️Otherwise under 10cm? StopThis is the band. Do nothing.
    4. ⬆️Otherwise drive forwardToo far — close the gap.
